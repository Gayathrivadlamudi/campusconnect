The keystore works like a digital signature or a fingerprint.
 It tells Google Play and the Android system exactly who built the app.
This stops hackers from creating a fake version of your app and pretending it is yours.

and there are closed testing and also open testing
All three testing stages have the same goal — finding bugs before releasing your app.
The difference is WHO gets access to test your application.

Internal Testing: You add testers using their email addresses or Google Groups. Maximum 100 testers.
Closed Testing: You create a tester list or Google Group and make the release available to those selected testers.
You can manage multiple testing tracks and larger groups.mainly u can select testers
Open: Wider audience can join voluntarily.

Your privacy policy should explain:
- What information you collect.

- Whether you share it with third parties.

What do you do?
1. Create a privacy policy that accurately describes your app.
